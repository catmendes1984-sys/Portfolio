Bug report: crash on loading screen after patch

GameStarfall Online (fictional)
SeverityHigh: affects access, reported by many players since patch 2.4.1
PlatformsPC (launcher v5.1), Windows 11. Not reproduced on console.
SummaryGame crashes to desktop at 80% on the loading screen when starting a saved campaign.
Steps to reproduce1. Launch game and sign in. 2. Select Continue Campaign. 3. Wait for the loading bar to reach 80%. Game closes with no error message.
Expected / actualCampaign loads to the hub / crash to desktop every time.
Evidence12 crash logs, 9 screenshots, device specs. Reinstall and file verification did not help.
WorkaroundStarting a new campaign works. Disabling cloud saves lets 6 of 8 tested players load.
Player impact140 tickets in 48 hours, roughly 3x the normal volume for this category.

Sample 3

Case study: spotting a repeat issue early

Problem. After a content update, a small number of cases described missing items that did not match any known issue.

Investigation. I grouped cases by platform, account region and purchase date, and found they all shared one store region and one purchase window.

Action. I escalated with a pattern summary and evidence, so developers could go straight to the likely cause, and wrote a macro so agents handled affected players the same way.

Result. The cause was confirmed quickly, affected players were restored, and repeat contacts dropped. (Fictional example for illustration.)

Sample 4

Knowledge-base article: "Game crashes when loading a save"

Before you start: check that your game and launcher are up to date.

  • Verify your game files through your launcher.
  • Update your graphics drivers and restart your PC.
  • Temporarily turn off cloud saves and try again.
  • Still crashing? Contact us with your crash log, your device specs and the time it happened, and we will pass it to the team.
